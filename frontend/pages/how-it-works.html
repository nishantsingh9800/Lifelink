Workflow & Safety Model

How LifeLink Coordinates Blood Emergencies

From verified request creation to 5-stage progressive escalation and hospital fallback.

Critical Safety Rule: Never delay urgent medical care while waiting for donor matching or software escalation. Always coordinate immediately with your treating hospital's transfusion service.

1. End-to-End Request Lifecycle

  1. Register & Verify: Users register as a Requester or Donor and verify their phone number.
  2. Submit Request: Requester specifies required blood group, units, hospital, city, and urgency.
  3. Rule-Based Prioritization: Candidate donors are ranked by availability, proximity, and verification.
  4. Multi-Donor Alerts: Multiple eligible candidates receive notifications with I CAN HELP or NOT AVAILABLE actions.
  5. Authorized Private Chat: Accepting a request unlocks a private chat between requester and donor without exposing phone numbers.
  6. Outcome Recording: Requests remain active or unresolved until genuine fulfilment, cancellation, or expiry is recorded.

2. Five-Stage Progressive Escalation

  1. Stage 1 — Nearby Potential Donors: Notify the closest suitable candidates first.
  2. Stage 2 — Additional Nearby Candidates: Expand to more matching candidates if responses are insufficient.
  3. Stage 3 — Wider Search Radius: Broaden geographical scope using configurable city/distance rules.
  4. Stage 4 — Blood-Bank Assistance: Surface verified directory listings and official resources such as e-RaktKosh.
  5. Stage 5 — Hospital-Coordinated Search: Guide immediate contact with the treating hospital blood bank and licensed centres while keeping the request tracked as unresolved.
