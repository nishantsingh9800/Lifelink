About LifeLink

Smart Blood Donor Matching & Emergency Response System

Why LifeLink Exists

During blood emergencies, families often rely on fragmented phone calls, WhatsApp forwards, and social media posts. This causes uncertain donor availability, uncoordinated follow-ups, and public exposure of personal phone numbers.

LifeLink brings donor prioritization, multi-donor alerts, progressive escalation, privacy-preserving chat, blood-bank discovery, and guided assistance into a single platform.

Clinical & Operational Boundaries

  • LifeLink does not fabricate donor records or blood-bank stock numbers.
  • Basic ABO/Rh group matching is preliminary only; crossmatching and donor eligibility are decided by licensed healthcare professionals.
  • The LifeLink Assistant uses rule-based platform workflows and is not a medical professional.
