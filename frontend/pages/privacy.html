Privacy Policy

What Data We Collect: Name, email, phone number, city, approximate location, optional date of birth, blood group (for donors), and blood-request coordination records.

Why We Collect It: Strictly to coordinate emergency blood-donor matching, notifications, authorized in-app communication, and platform safety moderation.

Location & Contact Privacy: Public donor search shows only city and approximate location. Phone numbers are never returned in public search APIs and are only revealed if both requester and donor give explicit mutual consent.

Access & Retention: Access is restricted via backend role authorization and Supabase Row Level Security (RLS). Users can request account deactivation or deletion through profile settings or administrators.
