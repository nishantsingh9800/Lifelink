Incoming Blood Request Alerts

Requests matched to your donor profile. Accepting opens a private in-app chat without automatically sharing your phone number.

Aadhaar Verification Required Before Donating

Verify your 12-digit Aadhaar once before accepting a blood donation request.

Mandatory Location Radius Sharing: Enabling browser location is required when accepting a blood request so the requester can see your approximate arrival zone (~1 km radius). Exact GPS coordinates are never stored or shared.

Live Face Verification Required to Accept

Confirm you are the registered donor before accepting this blood request. Capture a live selfie or upload a front-facing photo.
