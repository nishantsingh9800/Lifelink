Built for Safety, Privacy, and Accountability

Designed to replace scattered social forwards and unverified calls with structured coordination.

Privacy Protection

Privacy-First Communication

Phone numbers and exact residential addresses are never shown in donor search results. Private conversations open only between a requester and an accepting donor.

Rule-Based Priority

Transparent Donor Matching

Candidates are prioritized using transparent rules: availability status, city/proximity, verification state, and notification opt-in. Final eligibility rests with the blood bank.

Hospital Fallback

Honest Escalation Workflow

When donor responses are insufficient, LifeLink escalates to wider search and verified blood-bank directory resources without fabricating stock availability.

Supported ABO / Rh Blood Groups

Preliminary group matching only. Final compatibility, crossmatching, and screening are performed exclusively by licensed blood centres.
